FAQ

Common Questions

Do impact windows really eliminate the need for hurricane shutters?

Yes. All of our impact windows and doors are tested and approved to withstand large missile impact and cyclical wind pressure, meeting Miami-Dade County and Florida Building Code requirements without additional shutters.

Will impact windows lower my insurance premiums?

Many homeowners qualify for windstorm insurance discounts after installing approved impact windows and doors. We provide the documentation your insurer needs.

How long does installation take?

Most residential projects are completed within one to three days, depending on the number of openings and site conditions.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, we partner with financing providers to offer flexible payment plans so you can protect your home without paying everything upfront.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve homeowners and businesses throughout South Florida and beyond. Contact us to confirm service in your area.
